How to read this data
Current price
The price each station published to DGEG on the latest collection day. Stations that stopped publishing are left out; an old price is never repeated.
Median
The middle value when the stations' prices are sorted: half are cheaper, half dearer. It is the headline figure because a few unusual prices distort the mean.
Change
The median on the day shown minus the median 7 days earlier, with the same filters. It is hidden when either day has no prices, or when the number of stations differs by more than 20% between the two days (the change would reflect which stations published, not prices). Colours never mean “good” or “bad”.
P10–P90
The range holding 80% of prices: 10% of stations are below P10 and 10% above P90.
Dates
Each day is the collection day (UTC); prices are collected once a day. A station's DGEG update time is shown in Lisbon time and is for information only; it is only kept for the latest price.
Difference from DGEG
This is a simple median of stations. DGEG's official weekly average is weighted by sales and is usually 2–3% lower. Do not compare them directly.
Units
Most fuels are sold per litre; natural gas per kg or per m³. Units are never mixed, and only fuels sold per litre are compared on one chart.
Names
Fuel names are shown in English with DGEG's original Portuguese name alongside. Places, brands and station names are exactly as DGEG publishes them.
Limits
Station name, brand and address are the latest ones (no history). About 8% of stations carry the brand “Genérico”. Brands with fewer than 10 stations are not plotted in the brand comparison. Mainland Portugal only.

Source: Direção-Geral de Energia e Geologia (DGEG), station data published on dados.gov.pt (CC BY 4.0). Independent project, not affiliated with DGEG. Code and methodology.
